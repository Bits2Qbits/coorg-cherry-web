Meet the Estate

The Land

Kodagu, Karnataka — Western Ghats

Coorg — known locally as Kodagu — sits high in the Western Ghats, a Geographical Indication–certified coffee region that grows nearly 40% of India’s coffee between 1,000 and 1,750 metres above sea level. This is not a plantation in the industrial sense: coffee grows here under a living rainforest canopy of rosewood, silver oak, jackfruit and wild fig, intercropped with pepper, cardamom, orange and mango — a forest that produces, rather than a field that’s managed.

The Kodava people have farmed this land for generations, and Coorg Cherry’s founder is one of them — this is a family relationship with the estate, not a sourcing deal struck at a trade fair. Even our palette carries the inheritance: the burgundy and gold running through this page are drawn from the Kodava chale, the sash worn by Kodava men for centuries before we ever put it on a coffee bag.

The Coffee

Featured Estate: Chandragiri — Fermented Washed Arabica

One estate, curated pipeline. Currently featuring Chandragiri — a specialty-grade Arabica grown between 1,000 and 1,750 metres in Coorg’s rainforest canopy, washed and fermented before a slow sun-dry that lets the cup develop real complexity — cocoa and hazelnut up front, berry and citrus lifting through the middle, molasses and spice in the finish. We don’t cup it ourselves and tell you it’s exceptional — we let a roaster’s own cupping table decide that.

The People

No middlemen. Just the estate, the shipment, and us.

Coorg Cherry exists because its founder grew up on this land before he ever sold a bag of it. He’s Kodava — the same community that has tended these estates for generations — and the relationship behind every shipment is a family one, not a sourcing contract renegotiated each season. Direct trade, for us, means the grower is paid directly and fairly, with no importer’s markup sitting between the estate and the roaster who buys from us.
